Controls

        

          
WASD or ↑←↓→Move

          
MouseLook around

          
SpaceJump / swim up · double-tap to fly (creative)

          
ShiftSneak (won't fall off edges) / fly down

          
R or double-tap WSprint

          
Left clickBreak block (hold in survival)

          
Right clickPlace block

          
Middle clickPick block

          
1–9 / mouse wheelSelect hotbar slot

          
EInventory (creative block picker / survival crafting)

          
T / Enter / /Chat & commands (type /help)

          
F5 or VSwitch first / third person view

          
F1 · F2 · F3Hide HUD · Screenshot · Debug info

          
QDrop one item (survival)

          
EscPause menu
